# Free / FOSS Explore materials

All files in `assets/foss/` are **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** color maps from
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/) (formerly CC0Textures). No paid Asset Store
packs, no Minecraft assets, no scraped brand textures.
